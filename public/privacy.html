Privacy Policy

Motion Replicator is a personal, single-user task scheduling app. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses Google Calendar to:

  • read free/busy times from the owner's primary calendar, so tasks are planned around existing commitments;
  • create, update and delete events in a dedicated "Tasks" calendar that the app creates and manages.

The app never modifies the owner's other calendars or events.

What is stored

Tasks entered by the owner and a record of the calendar events the app created are stored in a private database. The Google refresh token is encrypted before it is stored. Free/busy data is used only while planning and is not kept.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can disconnect Google in the app, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
